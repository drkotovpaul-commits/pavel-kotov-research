# Notes and Hypotheses

This repository is a public research record.

Use `templates/NOTE_TEMPLATE.md` for research notes and `templates/HYPOTHESIS_TEMPLATE.md` for hypothesis entries.

Editorial principles:
- State questions precisely.
- Distinguish observation, interpretation, and hypothesis.
- Do not present untested propositions as established findings.
- Keep personal and confidential information out of public entries.
- Do not use the site as a substitute for peer-reviewed publication.
