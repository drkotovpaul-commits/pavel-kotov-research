# Pavel Kotov — Independent Research

Personal scientific record documenting the development of an independent research programme in movement, physiology, adaptation and related methodological questions.

**Researcher:** Pavel Kotov  
**Research programme:** Adaptive Movement Research  
**ORCID:** 0009-0007-0763-1218

---

## Purpose

This repository maintains the public scientific record associated with the independent research work of Pavel Kotov.

It documents the development of research questions, hypotheses, conceptual notes, scholarly outputs and scientific dialogue while preserving distinctions between:

- exploratory research development;
- working hypotheses;
- released preprints;
- manuscripts in development;
- journal-submission history;
- peer-reviewed publications.

The repository is not intended to present developing propositions as established scientific conclusions.

---

## Repository Scope

This repository serves as a **personal scientific record**.

It contains materials documenting the development and scholarly history of the research programme, while the dedicated **Adaptive Movement Research** website maintains the current programme-level research architecture.

These two functions are intentionally separated.

### Personal Scientific Record

This repository documents:

- research development;
- conceptual and methodological notes;
- hypotheses and propositions;
- publication history;
- released scholarly records;
- scientific dialogue and areas open to critique.

### Adaptive Movement Research

The dedicated Adaptive Movement Research project maintains:

- the current research architecture;
- Framework Paper development;
- programme-level methodology;
- research structure;
- canonical AMR publication records.

Current AMR website:

https://drkotovpaul-commits.github.io/Adaptive-movement-research/
