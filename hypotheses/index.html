Working Hypotheses

Candidate relationships for empirical investigation.

This register documents working hypotheses as they are formulated, operationalised, challenged and prepared for possible empirical testing.

Purpose

A working hypothesis is a testable possibility, not a conclusion.

The hypotheses recorded here represent provisional relationships within an evolving research programme.

Their inclusion indicates that a relationship is considered scientifically examinable. It does not indicate empirical confirmation.

Each hypothesis should ultimately be linked to operational definitions, measurable variables, competing explanations and conditions under which the proposition could fail.

Nomenclature

Working hypotheses are distinct from Framework Paper propositions.

This page uses the prefix WH for Working Hypothesis.

These working hypotheses belong to the evolving personal research register and may be revised as the programme develops.

They are separate from the formal research propositions presented in the Adaptive Movement Research Framework Paper.

Framework Paper propositions retain their own published nomenclature and should not be interpreted as equivalent to the WH sequence shown here.

Active Working Hypotheses

Current candidate relationships.

The current register focuses first on state-dependent physiological response and measurement feasibility before stronger claims concerning adaptation or downstream health relevance are considered.

WH-01 — State-Dependent Response

A comparable external movement-related demand may produce systematically different internal physiological response profiles when the same individual begins the exposure from different biological states.

Status · Operationalizing

WH-02 — Multivariable Internal Response

State-dependent internal demand may be represented more informatively by a prespecified multivariable physiological response profile than by one isolated response variable or one universal load score.

Status · Operationalizing

WH-03 — Temporal Response Structure

Synchronized measurements collected during exposure and recovery may reveal temporal response characteristics that are not captured by isolated pre-exposure or post-exposure measurements.

Status · Developing

WH-04 — Within-Person Reproducibility

At least some state-dependent response characteristics may be reproducible within an individual when baseline state, external demand and measurement conditions are sufficiently characterized.

Status · Developing

WH-05 — Movement Organisation as an Explanatory Layer

After current biological state and external demand have been characterized, measurable features of movement organisation may provide additional information about variation in the internal physiological response.

Status · Developing

WH-06 — Recovery Trajectory

Differences in acute response may be followed by distinguishable recovery trajectories that provide additional within-person information beyond the response observed during movement alone.

Status · Developing

WH-07 — Repeated Exposure

Repeated exposure under sufficiently characterized conditions may produce reproducible changes in response and recovery trajectories within the same individual.

Status · Downstream

WH-08 — Adaptive Interpretation

Reproducible longitudinal change across repeated exposure may support an adaptive interpretation only when acute response, recovery and measurement reliability have already been established.

Status · Downstream
Candidate Construct

State-Dependent Biological Load is not itself an established hypothesis result.

State-Dependent Biological Load is currently being developed as a working construct for describing the internal biological demand associated with the interaction among current state, external movement demand, movement organisation and context.

The construct provides a possible explanatory framework for several working hypotheses on this page.

It is not currently treated as a validated biological quantity, diagnostic score or established mechanism.

Its scientific usefulness depends on whether operational representations derived from it provide reproducible and testable information.

Hypothesis Dependency

Downstream hypotheses depend on earlier measurement relationships.

Adaptation cannot be inferred directly from a single movement episode. The programme therefore follows a dependency structure.

Defined baseline state → Defined external demand → Measurable internal response → Reproducibility → Recovery → Repeated exposure → Adaptation

Failure at an earlier stage limits or prevents stronger interpretation at later stages.

Falsifiability

What would count against the current hypothesis architecture?

No State Dependence

Comparable external demands do not show reproducible differences in internal response after relevant measurement error and contextual variation are considered.

No Added Multivariable Information

A multivariable response structure provides no useful or reproducible information beyond simpler established measurements.

No Reproducibility

Candidate response characteristics cannot be reproduced within individuals under adequately standardized conditions.

No Incremental Movement Information

Movement-organisation variables provide no additional explanatory information after current state and external demand are characterized.

Alternative Explanations

Apparent support may arise for reasons other than the proposed relationship.

Differences in physiological response may reflect measurement error, insufficient standardization, environmental conditions, circadian variation, prior activity, recovery status or other unmeasured factors rather than a meaningful state-dependent relationship.

Apparent relationships with movement organisation may also reflect external load differences rather than an independent explanatory contribution from movement characteristics.

Repeated changes may reflect familiarization, learning, instrumentation effects or regression toward the mean rather than biological adaptation.

These alternative explanations must therefore remain part of hypothesis testing rather than being treated only as secondary limitations.

Working Hypothesis Status

Status describes development, not scientific importance.

Developing

The proposed relationship remains conceptually or methodologically incomplete.

Operationalizing

Variables, conditions and measurement representations are being defined for a possible test.

Feasibility-Testable

The proposition has sufficient structure to permit a limited measurement or feasibility-stage test.

Downstream

The proposition depends on successful validation of earlier state, response, recovery or reproducibility relationships.

Revised

The proposition has been narrowed or reformulated following conceptual, methodological or empirical analysis.

Rejected

The proposed relationship is no longer retained because evidence or methodological analysis does not support its continued use.

Scientific Boundary

Working hypotheses are not established mechanisms or effects.

None of the WH propositions on this page should be interpreted as an established biological law, validated diagnostic relationship, demonstrated treatment effect or proven preventive mechanism.

Some propositions may not survive operationalization. Others may be contradicted by feasibility testing or later empirical evidence.

Rejection or narrowing of a working hypothesis is therefore considered a valid scientific outcome of the programme.
