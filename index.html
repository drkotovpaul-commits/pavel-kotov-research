This site documents ongoing work on human movement, dynamic biological state, and adaptation. It is an open space for research notes, hypotheses, publications, and scientific discussion.

My current research is centred on Adaptive Movement Research (AMR), an independent programme investigating the bidirectional relationship between movement organization and the internal biological state of the organism.
