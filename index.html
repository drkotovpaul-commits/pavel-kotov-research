Research Position

An independent scientific practice grounded in questions rather than conclusions.

Adaptive Movement Research investigates how movement organisation relates to dynamic biological response across multiple physiological systems.

The objective is not to propose immediate interventions, but to define measurable relationships that can later be examined empirically through collaborative scientific research.
