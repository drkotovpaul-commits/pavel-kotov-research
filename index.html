Research Position

Independent scientific practice grounded in measurable questions.

My work explores how movement organisation may reflect and influence the dynamic internal biological state of the human organism.

Rather than proposing immediate clinical solutions, the objective is to develop reproducible conceptual and methodological frameworks that can later be tested through collaborative scientific research.
