Purpose

A research notebook rather than a finished body of evidence.

These notes document ideas while they are being developed, examined and refined.

Their presence on this website does not imply that a proposition has been validated or that an observation represents an established scientific finding.

Observational Principle

Observation precedes interpretation.

Movement characteristics can be observed and measured, while their biological meaning remains an empirical question.

A movement observation alone should therefore not be treated as evidence of a specific physiological mechanism, causal relationship or clinical effect.

The working distinction between observation, measurement and interpretation is retained throughout the research programme.

Scientific Restraint

An evolving note is not an established result.

The notebook is intentionally designed to preserve uncertainty. Statements may remain tentative until they have been examined against appropriate evidence and alternative explanations.

Current notes should therefore be read as part of an evolving research process rather than as completed findings.
