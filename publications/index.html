Scholarly outputs and publication history.

This section records research outputs according to their actual scholarly status and distinguishes released preprints, continuing manuscript development, journal submission and formal peer-reviewed publication.

Publication Record

Publication status is kept separate from research development.

A released preprint, a manuscript in development, a journal submission and a peer-reviewed publication represent different scholarly states.

This record preserves those distinctions so that continuing development of the research programme does not retrospectively alter the status or content of an earlier scholarly output.

Detailed scientific architecture is maintained separately within Adaptive Movement Research and is not duplicated here as part of the bibliographic record.

Historical Record

The released preprint remains a fixed scholarly artifact.

Preprint v1.0 documents a specific stage in the development of Adaptive Movement Research.

Later manuscript development should be read as subsequent scientific development rather than as a silent revision of the released preprint.

Scientific Boundary

Publication status and evidential status are not interchangeable.

Public release of a manuscript establishes a scholarly record but does not by itself establish the validity of its scientific propositions.

Likewise, journal submission, editorial evaluation and continuing manuscript development should not be interpreted as empirical confirmation of the research framework.

Scientific claims remain dependent on the evidence appropriate to the specific relationship being examined.
