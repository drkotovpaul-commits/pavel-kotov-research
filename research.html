Adaptive Movement Research

Movement organization ↔ internal biological state

AMR is an independent research programme focused on whether movement organization can both reflect the dynamic internal state of the organism and contribute to subsequent physiological change.

Central question

Can movement organization provide measurable information about dynamic internal biological state, while movement itself functions as a recurrent biological perturbation that modifies subsequent multisystem physiological state?

Working architecture

  1. Current biological state
  2. Movement impulse
  3. Organization of movement
  4. Multisystem response
  5. Recovery / remodelling
  6. Repeated exposure
  7. Adaptation

The framework is intentionally bidirectional. No single movement variable or sensor is treated as a universal proxy for the whole organism.

Empirical direction

Measurement

Operationalize candidate movement variables and establish reproducibility.

Acute response

Test temporally synchronized movement and physiological relationships.

Recovery

Characterize recovery kinetics and within-person trajectories.

Adaptation

Examine systematic change across repeated exposure.
